# 4. Points (Portal Points)

#### 4.1 Current points

After the Badge Program went live:

* Daily point claims from the old quest era are gone
* **Points arrive weekly**, based on badges claimed
* The Portal leaderboard ranks users by total points

#### 4.2 Do points equal an airdrop?

**Not yet.** The team has framed points/badges as testnet incentives, sale priority, and Season 1 positioning. Any future $LNRA conversion is at the team’s discretion.
