# 5. Role and Xp

#### **5.1.** Role Information

Basic Role: **Lineran Role**

* **Role to obtain:** Lineran Role
* **Requirement:** **Hold 0.001 ETH** on the **ERC-20** network (Ethereum Mainnet).
* **How to obtain:** Visit [https://guild.xyz/linera](https://guild.xyz/linera), then connect your **Wallet** and **Discord** to obtain the Role.
* **Important note:** This is a **mandatory requirement** to unlock access to the **Testnet** and participate in XP-earning programs.

#### 5.3 Member Levels

The following are the available **Member Levels** and the amount of XP required to reach each level:

* **Bronze:** 1,525 XP (Level 5)
* **Silver:** 4,575 XP (Level 10)
* **Gold:** 26,700 XP (Level 20)
* **Platinum:** 267,000 XP (Level 50)

#### 5.4 Earning XP Through Games and Challenges

**A. Game Activities**

*   **Participate in Game Tours:** Held every Friday evening.

    → **Rewards:** Top 1–25 players earn XP. Winning **1st place 3 times** grants the **GameLord** role.
*   **Participate in Regional Mini Games:** Held from Monday to Sunday.

    → **Information:** Details will be updated by the Mods every Monday.

**B. Content Creation Challenges**

**How to earn XP:** Post **artwork, memes, or articles** related to the project.

#### 5.5. General Notes

**No XP is awarded for checking your rank:** Type the `!rank` command in the 🤖│check-your-rank channel.
