# 3. Badge Program

#### 3.1 4 Four badge types

| Badge          | What it appears to reward                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Achiever**   | Consistency, daily completion, goal-oriented play                   |
| **Killer**     | Win rate / PnL / beating other players                              |
| **Explorer**   | Trying many games and market types                                  |
| **Socializer** | Referral network on the Portal — invited wallets must be **active** |

Exact formulas are **unpublished**. Socializer looks at your referral network on the Portal.

`Base → Gold → Platinum → Diamond`

Total badges appear on your portfolio. Yesterday’s claimed badge can restyle your Share Card.

#### 3.3 Claim rules (important)

* Badges are **earned by trading on the App**
* Badges are **claimed on the Portal the next day**
* You can claim **only one badge type per day**
* Points are distributed **weekly**, based on claimed badges
* Not every day is eligible; community estimates suggest only a portion of daily activity qualifies

#### 3.4 Claim flow

1. Trade on the App during the day
2. Next day, open [https://portal.linera.net](https://portal.linera.net/?referralCode=noble-mantis-3791)
3. Open Badges
4. Select **one** eligible badge
5. Claim
6. Check portfolio + per-badge leaderboards

#### 3.5 What badges are used for?

* Identity / ranking during the testnet
* Weekly point distribution
* **Reserved Pool priority** in the $LNRA Community Round\
  Settlement order: Diamond → Platinum → Gold → Base → remainder to Open (pro rata)

A badge claimed today can still qualify for the reserved pool. You do not need to be an OG.
