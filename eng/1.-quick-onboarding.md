# 1. Quick onboarding

#### Step 1 — Open the App

1. Go to [https://app.linera.xyz/](https://app.linera.xyz/)
2. Click **Connect Wallet**
3. Sign in with your wallet (Dynamic-powered login)
4. The first connection **creates your Linera user chain**

New users typically receive **200 test coins**. About **15 coins** are distributed daily so you can keep playing. These coins are testnet-only, not real money.

#### Step 2 — Open the Portal

1. Go to [https://portal.linera.net](https://portal.linera.net/?referralCode=noble-mantis-3791)
2. Sign in / connect the **same wallet** used on the App
3. No transaction is required — it only proves wallet ownership
4. Check Badges, Points, and Leaderboard

#### Step 3 — Link community accounts (recommended)

* Follow [@linera\_io](https://x.com/linera_io) and [@lineraxyz](https://x.com/lineraxyz)
* Join Discord: https://discord.com/invite/linera
* Get the **Lineran** role if Portal/quests still require it
* Use the **same Discord account** on Guild (if used) and the Portal so the role maps correctly
